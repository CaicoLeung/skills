# 错误记录

## 2026-10-10 · Round 1 · A2 日常

### 第1句 我昨天下午跟朋友去看了电影。
| 错写 | 正解 | 标签 |
|------|------|------|
| wachted | watched | `spelling-typo` |
| watched a movie | saw a movie | `collocation-wrong-partner` |
| in the afternoon yesterday | yesterday afternoon | `time-adverbial-order` |

### 第2句 她每天早上七点起床，然后喝一杯咖啡。
| 错写 | 正解 | 标签 |
|------|------|------|
| get up | gets up | `third-person-s` |
| drink | drinks | `third-person-s` |
| everday morning | every morning | `everyday-vs-every-day` |

### 第3句 桌上有一个苹果和两个橘子，你吃吧。
| 错写 | 正解 | 标签 |
|------|------|------|
| There are an apple | There's an apple | `there-be-agreement-nearest` |
| you can free to taste | help yourself | `modal-mixed-structure` |
| three oranges | two oranges | `source-misread` |

### 第4句 我家离公司很近，走路十分钟就到了。
| 错写 | 正解 | 标签 |
|------|------|------|
| this's close between my home and company | My home is close to the office | `distance-close-structure` |
| can arrived | can get | `modal-plus-past-form` |
| arrived it | get there | `intransitive-verb-object` |
| （逗号直接连第二句） | — / and 重组 | `run-on-comma-splice` |

### 第5句 周末你打算做什么？我想在家休息。
| 错写 | 正解 | 标签 |
|------|------|------|
| on weekend | on the weekend / this weekend | `article-missing-the` |
| want taking | want to take | `infinitive-after-want` |
| reset | rest | `rest-vs-reset` |
